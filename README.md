# Project Halo

Project Halo adds a colored outline around your own character in RuneLite while a protection prayer is active. It provides a visual indication of the prayer you currently have enabled.

The default aura colors are:

- Protect from Melee: red
- Protect from Missiles: green
- Protect from Magic: blue

The aura disappears when no supported protection prayer is active. It applies only to your character, not other players or NPCs.

## How to use

With Project Halo installed in RuneLite, open the Plugins settings, search for **Project Halo**, and turn it on. In its settings, leave **Enable aura** enabled.

Activate a protection prayer normally in the game. The outline follows your active prayer automatically. Turn the prayer off to remove the outline.

## Settings

- **Enable aura:** Show or hide the outline without disabling the plugin.
- **Aura thickness:** Adjust the outline width from 1 to 12 pixels.
- **Aura opacity:** Adjust visibility from 0% to 100%. At 0%, the outline is invisible.
- **Prayer colors:** Choose a separate color for each of the three protection prayers.

The default outline is 4 pixels thick with 60% opacity.

Project Halo is visual only. It does not activate or switch prayers, recommend which prayer to use, or perform gameplay actions.
